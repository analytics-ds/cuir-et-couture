## Write to the editorial team

For a correction, a factual clarification, a story idea or a press enquiry, one address: **redaction@cuir-et-couture.fr**.

## Report an error

We correct factual errors quickly and display the update date on the page concerned. Send the article URL and the point to correct, with your source if you have one.

## Ateliers, houses and makers

You make, tan or cut: we are interested. Describe your work in a few lines, with a link to your pieces. No coverage is sold, and none is promised.
