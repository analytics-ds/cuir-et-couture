Stretch leather is the only major innovation in leather clothing in half a century. A very thin hide bonded onto an elastic backing: the garment follows the body instead of containing it. That is what made the leather dress wearable as a dress, and leather trousers wearable as jeans.

It is also a field where marketing moves faster than technique. This section separates genuine stretch leather from soft leather and from elastane-backed imitation.

## What you will find here

- **The definition**: what technically separates stretch leather from soft leather.
- **The making**: hide selection, splitting, bonding, elasticity rates.
- **The cut**: what stretch changes in pattern making, and why not everything can be sewn the same way.
- **The houses**: who makes it, since when, and on which garments.
