A leather jacket can leave a production line in thirty minutes or an atelier in thirty hours. The word couture separates those two worlds: clean pattern making, panels assembled flat, seams that hold the hide instead of pulling it, finishing that shows inside as much as outside.

This section documents the work of ateliers, the gestures, the trades, and the markers that let you assess a build without being in the trade.

## What you will find here

- **Pattern making**: why a hide is not cut like a fabric.
- **Assembly**: flat-felled seams, topstitching, linings, and what the inside of a garment reveals.
- **Made to measure**: how it works, what it changes, what it costs.
- **French manufacturing**: what origin claims actually cover.
