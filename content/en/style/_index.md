Leather is the most loaded material in a wardrobe: it carries rock, motorcycling, couture and evening wear all at once. Well cut, a leather piece dresses an entire silhouette. Badly cut, it freezes it.

This section looks at leather as clothing rather than as material: which cuts work, which pieces outlast seasons, which houses hold their level year after year.

## What you will find here

- **The pieces**: dress, trousers, jacket, biker, jumpsuit, skirt, and what each one demands.
- **The silhouettes**: how to wear leather without turning it into a costume.
- **The houses**: our selections and comparisons, competitors included, on real merit.
- **Price markers**: what a buyer actually pays for.
